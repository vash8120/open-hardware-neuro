Positioning hardware for placing electrodes in fly and moth preparations.

## Stick-slip nanopositioner
- Based on En Te Hwu's design
- Piezo stack + magnet driven with a sawtooth wave from an audio amplifier
- Effectively "unlimited" travel range
- Step size: 30 nm – 2400 nm
- **Cost: ~$35 per axis**

## Parallel 3-axis micromanipulator
- Based on DiffractionLimited's closed-loop design
- Range of motion: 2 cm
- Reliable sub-micron stepping (50 nm theoretical limit)
- **Cost: ~$150**
