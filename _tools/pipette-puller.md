A novel, low-cost micropipette puller for pulling borosilicate electrodes.

## Novel design
- Nichrome heater
- Lead screw + solenoid pull mechanism
- Borosilicate glass only
- K-type thermocouple for temperature feedback
- STM32 microcontroller
- Magnetic encoder for position sensing
- **Cost: ~$100**
