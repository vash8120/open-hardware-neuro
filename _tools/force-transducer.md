Force transducers for measuring the flight forces of tethered *Manduca sexta*
(hawkmoth) — a low-cost approach we think of as "behavioral electrophysiology."

## Single-axis transducer
- Measures hawkmoth yaw / sideslip
- Tunable sensitivity
- Strain-gauge based
- **Cost: ~$15**

## Multi-axis transducer
- Maltese-cross design for multi-component force measurement
- Requires calibration
- **Cost: ~$90**
