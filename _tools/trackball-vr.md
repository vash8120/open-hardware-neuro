A virtual-reality trackball rig for walking fruit flies (*Drosophila
melanogaster*), with closed-loop visual feedback.

## Key components
- Holocube VR environment (Pyglet)
- FicTrac ball tracking with a PS3 Eye camera
- NEC M323W DLP projector
- Air-supported ball cast with Foam-iT! 3 and Ecoflex molds
- 3D-printed frame with wood dowels
- Side mirrors + back-projection screens for a wraparound display

**Cost: ~$115**
