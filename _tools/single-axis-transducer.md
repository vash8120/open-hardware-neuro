A single-axis strain-gauge force transducer for measuring the flight forces of
tethered *Manduca sexta* (hawkmoth) — a low-cost approach we think of as
"behavioral electrophysiology."

## Single-axis transducer
- Measures hawkmoth yaw / sideslip
- Tunable sensitivity
- Strain-gauge based
- **Cost: ~$15**
